# AI Stock Assist — Core Positioning Block (reusable, claims-checked)
**Use on:** landing page, About, directory listings, AEO answer pages, announcement §"What this means for you"
**Rule:** every differentiator below is structural and screenshot-provable. No superlatives, no predictions, no advisor-replacement claims.

## The one-paragraph version
AI Stock Assist compresses the research legwork of fundamental stock analysis — pulling the figures, computing the ratios, cross-checking the periods and currencies, and organizing it all into a decision-ready card — from hours per ticker into about a minute for up to ten at once. It supports more than one investment framework on the same verified data, names exactly what it couldn't verify instead of guessing, and shows the arithmetic behind every verdict so you can check it yourself. It doesn't predict prices and it isn't financial advice — it's assisted expertise that gets you to the informed-judgment stage faster, with facts that are grounded, checked, and dated.

## What it does for you (benefits, concrete)
- **Hours into minutes.** Ten tickers, fully worked — fundamentals, cash-flow quality, balance-sheet health, growth, verdict, and the exact conditions that would change it — in roughly the time it takes to pour a coffee. The same legwork done by hand means pulling statements, normalizing periods, converting currencies, and computing thirty-plus ratios per name.
- **One set of facts, multiple decision styles.** Value, growth, income, and momentum investors read the same card differently — so the card serves all of them: framework verdicts for the disciplined, raw verified metrics for the do-it-yourselfers, watch levels for the patient, and Wall Street's consensus alongside for contrast.
- **A second opinion that shows its work.** Every verdict decomposes into named checks with visible thresholds. "BUY" is never a vibe — it's six checks you can recount on your fingers.
- **Your watch levels, precomputed.** Every HOLD and SELL states the exact price or cash-flow figure at which the verdict would flip — arithmetic, not forecast. Circle the number, set your patience, move on.
- **Honesty about gaps.** When data is missing, the card scores what verified, names what didn't and why, and lowers its stated confidence. You always know how much weight the verdict can bear.

## Unlike most tools in this category (structural, verifiable)
- Every card carries **one dated data period and one dated currency conversion** — cross-checked before render, so mixed-period and mixed-currency ratios can't reach you.
- Missing data is **never scored as bad data and never hidden** — gaps are named on the card, per metric, with reasons.
- The plain-English narrative is **validated against the computed verdicts** before it renders — the prose cannot contradict the arithmetic.
- Every definition and threshold lives in **one versioned public dictionary** ("Definitions v1.0" in the footer) — the guide, the sidebar, and the scoring engine cannot disagree.
- Every score is **reproducible by a reader with a spreadsheet** from what's printed on the card — and archived internally in an append-only, version-stamped log.
- More than one framework — including **Graham Classic, the 1949 criteria unmodified** — can examine a stock, and disagreements between lenses are shown as information, not smoothed over.

## The journey framing (for About / longer copy)
Think of it as an accelerated apprenticeship: the card does in a minute what an analyst's first afternoon on a ticker produces, then hands you the judgment work that was always yours — horizon, temperament, position, conviction. The Metrics Guide teaches every concept in plain English as you go, so the tool makes you faster *and* more fluent. The card does the arithmetic; you make the decision.
