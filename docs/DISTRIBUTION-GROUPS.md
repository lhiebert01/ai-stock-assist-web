# Distribution Group Map — LinkedIn & Facebook

Reusable list of groups for syndicating the *Investing in the Intelligence Era* series.
**Fit = how well a group's audience matches a given episode.** The series is **value / long-term
stock investing** — so value & general stock groups are bullseyes; real-estate, forex/futures
trading, and ESG groups are off-theme for most episodes.

**Posting hygiene (both platforms):** space group posts out (don't blast all at once), **vary the
opening line** per group, and prefer **link in the first comment** (better reach + avoids spam
flags). Moderated groups queue posts for **admin approval** — normal; just wait.
