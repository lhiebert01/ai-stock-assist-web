## 🅐 HERO — "The Two Maps" (Substack header + social promo)

**Overlay at design time:** tagline *"Two maps. Both right. Pick your journey."* + AI Stock Assist diamond watermark, bottom-right (match the Ep 5 goose treatment). The generated file is clean (no text).

**Prompt:**

> A lone traveler stands at a rural crossroads at golden hour, holding two unfolded paper maps — one in each hand — glancing from one to the other. Both maps are clearly hand-drawn by experts but show different routes. A weathered wooden fingerpost sign points in two opposite directions; the dusty country road forks left and right into rolling hills under a warm, low sun. The mood is a quiet, pivotal decision — calm, cinematic, a little wistful. Leave clear headroom in the bottom third for a caption overlay.
>
> Real-world editorial photography, studio-grade quality. Shot on a Sony A7R V with a 50mm prime lens at f/8 (environmental scene). Natural golden-hour lighting. Tack-sharp focus throughout — deep depth of field, every detail crystal-clear from foreground to background. NO blurred backgrounds, NO bokeh — every fold of the maps, the wood grain of the signpost, each blade of grass visible. Real-people aesthetic — natural skin texture with visible pores, slight asymmetry, real wrinkles, hair flyaways, lived-in clothing. Must look like an actual professional photograph — NOT a 3D render, NOT an AI generation, NOT a painting. Strictly avoid AI tells: no plastic-glossy skin, no perfect symmetry, no glassy eyes, no surreal lighting. Reference style: Steve McCurry / Sebastião Salgado documentary photography.
