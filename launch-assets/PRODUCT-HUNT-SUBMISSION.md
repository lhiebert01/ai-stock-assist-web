A step-by-step run-through of Product Hunt's submission + launch process, tuned for a **fintech / AI** launch.
URL: https://aistockassist.com · Maker: Lindsay Hiebert (PI GenAI)

Core facts: **3 free analyses, no card** → credit packs from **$4.99 / 20 (~25¢ each)** and **$9.99 / 50**, no subscription.

## Read this first — set the goal correctly

PH ranking is momentum- and network-driven in the first hours. Two things in our favor vs a quiz/education app:
**AI + fintech is much closer to PH's native maker/tech/indie-investor audience**, so channel-fit is better here.
Still, treat PH as a **credibility + dofollow backlink + maker-network** play, not a user-acquisition firehose. A great PH
day = a badge, a few hundred targeted visitors, honest reviews, and early adopters. Your real volume comes from
**Reddit (our highest-leverage channel), the Substack series, and investing communities.**

**The launch is won BEFORE you publish.** The four things small launches skip — (1) a "Coming soon" teaser collecting
"Notify me" followers, (2) ~2 weeks of genuine PH engagement to build account karma, (3) a real list of supporters who
**already have** PH accounts, (4) a strong first-comment story — are what drive the first-hour momentum. Votes from
brand-new accounts created just to upvote you are discounted by PH's spam filter.

## PHASE 1 — Pre-launch (the decisive 1–2 weeks)
1. **Polish your PH account:** real photo, one-line bio, link to aistockassist.com. Aged + active accounts count; blank ones don't.
2. **Engage daily ~10 days:** upvote + leave thoughtful comments on 3–5 launches/day (especially fintech/AI tools). Builds karma AND reciprocity — the single biggest lever.
3. **Create a "Coming Soon" page** on PH (add product, set a future launch date) → publishes with a "Notify me" button = your first-hour upvote engine.
4. **Drive your audience to it** (Substack list, LinkedIn, X, email) to collect "Notify me" followers. Goal: **50–150+** before launch.
5. **Line up 15–30 real supporters who already have PH accounts** (DM personally). Ask them to "check it out and leave honest feedback" on launch morning — never "upvote me" or offer incentives (gets votes nuked).
6. **Prepare all assets below** (tagline, description, topics, gallery, first comment, optional video).

## PHASE 5 — Launch-day runbook (all times Pacific)
- **12:01 AM PT** — Product goes live (schedule it; you don't need to be awake).
- **When you wake** — Post the maker first comment. Verify page, links, gallery render.
- **Morning** — Notify channels in **WAVES** (don't dump at once): "Notify me" followers fire automatically → Substack list → LinkedIn → X → relevant communities. Say "we're live / would love your feedback," never "please upvote."
- **All day** — Reply to EVERY comment within minutes; offer to run a free analysis on a commenter's ticker. Ask happy users for an honest review (reviews factor into ranking).
- **~12:00–1:00 PM PT** — Second push (Europe evening + US midday).
- **~5:00–7:00 PM PT** — Final push before the day closes at 11:59 PM PT.

## Honest expectation-setting
Done well, PH for a fintech/AI tool should outperform a niche education app — the audience overlaps more. Expect a badge,
a few hundred to low-thousands of targeted visitors, honest reviews, and early adopters. Don't measure success only in
signups: take the badge, the dofollow backlink, and the maker feedback, and put user-acquisition energy into **Reddit,
the Substack "Intelligence Era" series, and investing communities** where your actual audience lives.
